## 1. Foundational Axioms and Definitions

**L0. Axiom (modeling): global identity.**
$$
x=x=1
$$
The universe, taken as a singular undivided whole, is assigned the value 1. The identity $x=x$ is a tautology; the assignment is a choice of unit.
Status: Axiom (modeling), a normalization convention. It carries no physical content beyond fixing the unit.

**LD1. Definition (relativistic block universe, operational).**
The linear flow of time $t$ is not treated as fundamental. Spacetime is handled as a fixed four-dimensional geometric block for the purposes of local measurement latching.
Status: Definition (operational stance).

**LD2. Definition (language as dielectric modulator).**
Language is a high-bandwidth, multi-timescale modulator of the dielectric medium. In this framework it is modeled as acting on attentional bias, on bioelectric pattern memory across the P-V-C triad, on chromatin residue, and on inter-organism phase-locking.
Status: Definition. The chromatin and phase-locking effects are claims, and are carried as L10 and L3 below.

**LD3. Definition (linguistic shutter and bookkeeping equation).**
$$
X+Y=1
$$
A coherent linguistic token fixes the micro-floor $Y$ (the smallest resolvable semantic unit) and its complementary macro-ceiling $X=1-Y$.
Status: Definition.

## 2. Phase-Space and Developmental Definitions

**LD4. Definition (Takens embedding for the linguistic-physiological state).**
The phase-space manifold $\mathcal{M}$ is reconstructed from pulsatile endocrine and neural time series by delay embedding.
Status: Definition (a standard technique applied inside the framework; exploratory for physiological data).

**LD5. Definition (behavioral archetypes via Jacobian eigenvalues).**
Classification of $\mathcal{M}$ into stable node, stable focus, limit cycle, and strange attractor according to the eigenvalues of the system Jacobian (the regime table of v9.0 Section 3).
Status: Definition.

**LD6. Definition (dual-engine mapping).**
The processing substrate is mapped to two parallel progenitor lineages with distinct chromatin configurations: an Otx2-derived engine (forebrain and midbrain) and a Gbx2-derived engine (hindbrain). The labels are names for the abstract sectors `otx_like` and `gbx_like` of [`finite_descent_claims.md`](finite_descent_claims.md) (FD7).
Status: Definition (developmental mapping, by label only).
Note: the progenitor populations are mutually exclusive; the adult structures are of course adjacent. The study does not describe either lineage as flexible or rigid.

## 4. Conjectures

**L1. Conjecture (block universe and P/NP).**
In the block universe, problem and solution coexist at fixed coordinates, so the computational asymmetry between discovery and verification collapses at the level of the shutter.
Status: Conjecture. Not used in any derivation of v10.0. The inference does not follow from the block-universe premise, because complexity classes concern the scaling of algorithmic resources; see v10.0 §3.3 and Aaronson (2005). Restored from the working draft, where it appeared as a grounding axiom; it was absent from the first claims draft.

**L2. Conjecture (gamma-band latch rate).**
The execution rate of the shutter is set by phase-locked gamma-band synchronization at 30–80 Hz.
Status: Conjecture. The mainstream account ties gamma to fine temporal granularity and syllable- and phrase-scale parsing to slower rhythms (Giraud and Poeppel 2012). Protocol 85.

**L3. Conjecture (language couples the two engines).**
Language is the high-bandwidth channel coupling the `otx_like` and `gbx_like` engines. Cortical speech rhythms and brainstem-driven respiratory and orofacial rhythms stay phase-related during speech.
Status: Conjecture. Speech does require both systems; the coupling form is the claim. Protocols 86, 89.

**L4. Conjecture (breakdown of coupling).**
A breakdown of cross-engine linguistic synchronization produces a discontinuous change in the reconstructed manifold.
Status: Conjecture. Protocol 86.

**L5. Conjecture (symbolic gating as syndrome decoding).**
The Rank-3 base realizes the syndrome space of the [7,4] Hamming code (eight elements: seven Fano points and zero), and symbolic gating acts as syndrome decoding on the final fold.
Status: Conjecture. The combinatorial facts are verified in `src/qdter/descent.py`; the identification with the QD-TER base is not. It carries a tension with D1 (the 36-mode waist) and an unproven link to D8. Protocol 87.

**L6. Conjecture (origin of the Valley).**
Sustained linguistic input that is intense and only partially resonant with the ordered cascade produces frozen partial resonance, with the internal shutter rate dropping below the lock-in threshold and the `gbx_like` engine freezing into hyper-rigid postural and vocal tone.
Status: Conjecture. Protocols 85, 89.

**L7. Conjecture (origin of the Abyss).**
High-valence symbolic saturation lets the predictive `otx_like` engine outrun grounding, producing compulsive projection and hallucinatory noise.
Status: Conjecture. Protocols 85, 89.

**L8. Conjecture (pineal–DMT phase-locking buffer).**
The pineal matrix (melatonin as a low-frequency clock, endogenous tryptamines including DMT as a high-frequency waveguide), acting via Sigma-1, functions as the primary phase-preserving temporal buffer that maintains Ridge residence and prevents decompression errors.
Status: Conjecture (extends beyond current empirical support).
Evidence: DMT binds the sigma-1 receptor (Fontanilla et al. 2009). The rat brain can synthesize and release DMT at concentrations comparable to monoamines, and cortical extracellular DMT was similar with and without a pineal gland (Dean et al. 2019). That result bears on the source of brain DMT, not on the proposed buffering function, which has not been tested. No function of endogenous DMT in human language is established.

**L9. Conjecture (adrenochrome flashpoint witness).**
Isolated in-situ adrenochrome serves as the high-frequency, localized witness of catastrophic decompression failure and collapse of zero-drift tension after the buffer is overloaded. Macro-indices (cortisol, HRV, cytokines, $\mathrm{Re}_\varepsilon$) are downstream systemic echoes.
Status: Conjecture (high-risk; retained per framework decision).
Evidence: adrenochrome is an oxidation product of epinephrine. The Hoffer–Osmond hypothesis concerned adrenochrome as a cause of psychosis and was not sustained; that is a different claim, and a marker role does not require psychoactivity. Nothing cited here measures adrenochrome in the proposed role, and no validated in-vivo assay is cited. A measurable proxy would be needed to test the conjecture.

**L10. Conjecture (language writes chromatin).**
Repeated linguistic input, through repetition and social reinforcement, writes into chromatin residue, the slow belief layer.
Status: Conjecture. The established analog is the effect of maternal behavior on glucocorticoid-receptor methylation in rodents (Weaver et al. 2004); a language-specific effect is not established. Protocols 88, 90.

**L11. Conjecture (feedback saturation).**
When the latency between action and reaction approaches zero, individual shutter cycles are bypassed, and the social construction acts as a low-pass filter admitting only degraded consensus.
Status: Conjecture. No protocol yet.

## 5. Invariants

**LI1. Axiom (modeling): transience of language-induced regimes.**
Language compression and symbolic-interface regimes remain transient relative to the light-entrained timescale. A regime induced by language input relaxes toward its pre-exposure state within a circadian cycle once the input ceases. A shift that persists across several cycles is not gating; it is evidence of residue writing (L10).
Status: Axiom (modeling). Testable corollary in Protocol 90.
