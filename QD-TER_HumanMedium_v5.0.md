## 12. Explicit Caveats and Limitations

1. **This document is speculative.** It extends the QD-TER Human-Medium framework into conjectural territory. All mechanisms require experimental validation.
2. **The seven-engine manifold is a theoretical ansatz.** The specific assignment of brain regions to electrodynamic roles is a framework-specific hypothesis that must be tested against lesion, stimulation, and imaging data.
3. **The coherence gradient (§3.1)** is a theoretical construct. The correlation between number of twist-locked engines and consciousness quality has not been empirically demonstrated.
4. **Capacity III (macro-somatic projection)** has the weakest empirical foundation. Cross-systemic entrainment is predicted by coherent electrodynamics but has not been demonstrated at the epigenetic level.
5. **Native AuNCs** are documented in trace-element literature but their functional role as dielectric tuning elements is a hypothesis. The ~3 ppb body burden may be insufficient for measurable biological effect.
6. **PAH effects** are dose-dependent and context-dependent. At low environmental exposure levels, PAHs may act as mild dielectric modulators rather than severe disruptors. The chronic high-dose effects described in §7.2 represent the upper bound of the dose-response curve.
7. **Phase III protocols** (especially holotropic hyperventilation) carry inherent risks. The safety constraints of v4.0 §6.5 apply without exception.
8. **No medical claims are made.** This is a theoretical physics-biology formalization. Any practical application requires professional medical supervision.
9. **Fröhlich condensates:** Coherent Fröhlich condensates at physiological temperatures remain debated. The document's claims are consistent with the weak-condensate regime; strong coherent condensates likely require non-biological conditions.
10. **Environmental acoustic coupling (§8.4)** is a novel hypothesis. While fungal mycelial networks and certain peptides demonstrate acoustic-dependence, the extension to human brain ECM requires direct experimental validation.
