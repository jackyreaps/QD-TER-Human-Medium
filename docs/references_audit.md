# Reference Audit

**Date:** 19 October 2026
**Scope:** All reference lists in `suite/`, `theory/`, `docs/`, and `adjacent/`. This audit records which citations were checked against independent sources, what was corrected, and what still needs checking before the suite is cited formally.

## Method

Each checked citation was looked up on the web and compared with at least one independent source (publisher page, PubMed or PMC, or institutional record). "Verified" means the paper exists and the author list, year, journal, and title match. It does not mean the paper's findings were assessed or that the framework's use of them was checked.

Before the fixes below, the reference lists contained 217 entries with a year. Of the 43 dated 2025–2026 (some repeated across documents), **three papers were checked**. Everything else is listed below as unchecked.

**Note on Kim et al. (2026).** The paper is real and the citation was accurate, but it is not settled science. A published erratum exists, and a physicist quoted in *New Scientist* (30 April 2026) called the reported response "incredibly implausible", as relayed by Microwave News. Image-duplication concerns on PubPeer were also reported in secondary coverage. The v2 text previously called the paper "experimentally verified"; it now describes it as a single, unreplicated report. No independent replication was found in the sources checked.

## Structural defect fixed

The v8 bibliography was two lists joined together (numbered 1–35, then restarting at 19). The second list repeated entries from the first, in several cases with wrong years: Brakedal, Christen, Conze, Martens, Okabe, Yi, Yoshino, plus duplicates of Dagum, Hainke, Hauglund, and Schimmelpfennig. Thirteen entries were removed: eleven were duplicates of entries already in the first list, and two (Igarashi 2025, Liao 2025) were cited nowhere in the text and carried the same suspect dating. Six entries that appeared only in the second list and are cited in the text (de la Iglesia, Dolev, Guala, Helfrich-Förster, Kim S., Zenchenko & Breus) were kept and the list was renumbered 1–41.

## Recommendations

1. Pick one citation style (for example APA 7, author–year) and give every entry a DOI.
2. Treat the "Not yet checked" table as a to-do list before any formal submission. Several of these entries are the evidence for specific claims in the regimen sections of v8, so a wrong date or title there weakens the argument it supports.
3. Where the framework leans on a single contested source (Kim et al. 2026), state the status in the text, as v2 now does.
