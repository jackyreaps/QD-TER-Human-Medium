**Human trial evidence (2025–2026):**

In overweight postmenopausal women with prediabetes, 250 mg/day for 10 weeks increased muscle insulin sensitivity (Yoshino et al.). 600–900 mg/day for 60 days improved 6-minute walk distance in midlife/older adults (Yi et al.).

**Mitochondrial ultraweak photon emission (UPE):**

- **Excited carbonyl species** during the Krebs cycle
- **Reactive oxygen species (ROS)** in the electron transport chain, particularly at Complex I and III
