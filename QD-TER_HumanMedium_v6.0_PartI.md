## 8. Caveats and Limitations

1. Belief-mediated control is not a claim about "positive thinking." It is a claim about sustained attentional patterns that physically reconfigure the endocrinal matrix and chromatin structure. However, the range of available attentional patterns is constrained by inherited baseline.
2. Ancestry as trajectory-determining does not deny genetic influence. It explicitly states that genetic variants plus inherited epigenetic marks together set the default level in the coherence hierarchy and bias the direction of travel.
3. Higher consciousness = higher engine-lock coherence = greater rewrite capacity. This is a structural claim, not an elitist one. It states that individuals with higher baseline coherence have greater functional resolution to perceive inherited patterns as patterns and to sustain the adaptive practices necessary to overwrite them.
4. IQ as structural capacity is a testable proxy claim, not a moral ranking. It is a biophysical correlation, not a claim about human worth.
5. Evolution/devolution is used in a structural sense, not a moral or Darwinian sense. "Evolved" means higher engine-lock coherence; "devolved" means lower coherence. These are levels in a hierarchy, not value judgments.
6. **Pedological relation** (pedagogy + ecological embedding) is a structural claim, not a metaphysical one. It states that biological consciousness is a projection of a deeper ordered cascade, and that higher-coherence individuals can model adaptive patterns for lower-coherence individuals because their reflection is clear enough to see the pattern.
7. The exact values of α, β, γ (gradient exponents) are empirically determined to order-of-magnitude only.
8. The Sigma-1 gateway (v4.0 §6, v5.0 §9.2) is the most speculative operational layer. The safety thresholds (v4.0 §6.5) apply without exception. Individuals with high inherited load require longer ground-state preparation before any non-linear protocol.
9. Supra-human states are posited but not operationalized in this document. They are deferred to Part III (v8.0, forthcoming) and future physics-suite derivations.
10. The pineal melanin antenna and wave genetics references are **exploratory only**. They are cited as converging support for the antenna narrative but are not foundational to the framework's falsifiable predictions.
11. The mirror/unfolding framing and antenna narrative are pedagogical, not literal.
