## 1. Source of the Integer 8

### 1.1 Residual waist

    N_waist = 36

The waist is the 36-mode residual block that survives the hierarchical
reduction.

### 1.2 Residual parity group

After the hierarchical reduction (12 → 6 → 3), the residual action that
remains on the waist is

    V_4 ≅ Z_2 × Z_2

generated by two commuting involutions:

- σ — residual parity
- τ — residual gap-2 shift

with

    dim_{F_2}(V_4) = 2,    |V_4| = 4.

### 1.3 Waist-mode projection

The action of V_4 is restricted to the 36-mode waist by the projector
Π_waist. Without this restriction the channel count would be over the full
space, not the protected residual sector. The count 8 is a statement about
the V_4 action on Π_waist · (full space), not on the full space directly.

### 1.4 Four orientation frames

The residual embedding admits four orientation frames on the waist block —
the minimal set compatible with the fold hierarchy and the self-dual locus.

### 1.5 Channel count

    N_channels = N_frames × dim_{F_2}(V_4) = 4 × 2 = 8.

Equivalently: four frames, each carrying the two generators of V_4, give
eight residual channels.

**Note on notation.** Do not write 8 = 4 × (2 × 2); that evaluates to 16.
The correct multiplier is the F_2-dimension of V_4 (which is 2), not the
group order |V_4| (which is 4).
