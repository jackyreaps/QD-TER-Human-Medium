    N_waist = 36

  are inherited.

## Part 2 — Conditional FEP Reduction

### 2.1 Scope

A **local, conditional** reduction. Not a claim that FEP is an unconditional
limiting case of the published QD-TER suite.

> Under the fast-aether limit, the generative-model assumption, and the
> endocrinal-matrix consistency condition, the linearized projected QD-TER
> dynamics is identical to the linearized Laplace FEP recognition dynamics
> in a neighborhood of the posterior well.

### 2.2 Axioms

**Axiom D.** dΨ/dt = −L_H Ψ + M_E F(Ψ, f), with cubic reaction
F(Ψ, f) = f Ψ (Ψ_A − Ψ)(Ψ − Ψ_B). Operators proposed in the human-medium layer.

**Axiom S.** L_H has a spectral gap bounded below by a Cheeger-type
expression, so the fast-aether limit is well-defined as λ_2 ≫ λ_slow.

**Axiom G.** The system implements p(o, s) and q(s) = N(μ, Σ) with μ = Ψ_s
and Π_o = f Ψ_A Ψ_B, Π_s = f Ψ_B (Ψ_B − Ψ_A). Not derived from the published
suite.

**Axiom C.** M_E = G Ψ_B / (Ψ_B − Ψ_A). Derived constraint under the chosen
precisions, not an independent biophysical measurement.

### 2.3 The Local Reduction Theorem

**Theorem.** Let Axioms D, S, G, C hold. For perturbations δΨ_s small enough
that the cubic remainder is negligible,

    δΨ̇_s = −Γ (Π_o + Π_s) · δΨ_s + O(ε)

identical to the linearized FEP recognition dynamics

    δμ̇ = −Γ (Π_o + Π_s) · δμ + O(ε)

under the identification μ = Ψ_s.

**Proof sketch (5 lines).**

1. Apply P_s. Since P_s L_H = 0 on the slow subspace, the Laplacian drops out.
2. Expand F around Ψ_B: F(Ψ_B, f) = 0, linear term −U''(Ψ_B) δΨ_s.
3. Γ_eff = P_s M_E U''(Ψ_B).
4. Under Axiom C, Γ_eff = Π_s G · f Ψ_B².
5. FEP rate Γ (Π_o + Π_s) = Π_s G · f Ψ_B² under Axioms G and C. Match.

Full derivation: reduction_fep_derivation.md.

### 2.4 Boundary of the Claim

- **Global fixed-point alignment:** not claimed. Under the natural assignment,
  μ* and Ψ_B differ by a term proportional to Ψ_B − Ψ_A. Equality requires
  Ψ_A = Ψ_B, which collapses the double-well structure. Alignment holds when
  P_s Ψ_B lies on the FEP manifold; this is a codimension-1 condition on the
  well parameters and is not generic.
- **Full nonlinear identity:** not claimed. The projected reaction is cubic;
  the FEP gradient is linear. Away from Ψ_B they differ by higher-order terms.
- **Unconditional recovery from the published suite:** not claimed. Axioms G
  and C are extra modeling assumptions.

## Part 3 — Non-Dual Adaptation Layer

### 3.1 Scope

A **proposed additional layer**, orthogonal to the FEP reduction. Conditional
on the physics suite supplying a Riemannian metric on the human-medium
parameter space. Without that metric, the layer is a formal structure without
content.

### 3.2 Axioms

**Axiom M.** The world-model parameter space carries a Riemannian metric
g_ij(x) derived from QD-TER quantities (cognitive glue, Re_ε, endocrinal
matrix norm).

**Axiom V.** There exists a vector field V on this manifold, transported
along update trajectories.

**Axiom Ω.** The scalar Ω = g_ij V^i V^j is the metric norm of V.

### 3.3 The Parallel Transport Condition

    ∇_X V = 0

expands in components to

    ∂V^k/∂x^i + Γ^k_{ij} V^j X^i = 0

with Γ^k_{ij} the Christoffel symbols of the metric g_ij.

### 3.4 Holonomy

On a curved manifold, parallel transport is path-dependent. Under a closed
loop γ,

    V(after loop) = H_γ · V(before loop)

where H_γ ∈ SO(d) is the holonomy matrix of the loop. The intentionality
vector is covariantly constant along the update trajectory. Under closed
loops it transforms by the holonomy, which is a measurable geometric phase.

### 3.5 Violation Detection

An update trajectory that does not satisfy the parallel transport condition
produces a nonzero covariant derivative:

    ‖∇_X V‖ ≠ 0

This quantity is measurable and can be monitored. A policy that samples
‖∇_X V‖ along the update path can identify trajectories that fail to preserve
the invariant direction.

### 3.6 What Is Not Claimed

- That the intentionality vector is a fixed vector in R^d. It is a section of
  a vector bundle over the parameter manifold.
- That Ω < 1 registers as a dielectric grid break. The map from Ω to the
  dielectric pipeline is a proposed modeling bridge, not a derived result.
- That the metric bridge in §3.7 is derived from the physics suite.

### 3.7 Metric Bridge (To Be Supplied)

    g_ij(x) = δ_ij · 1/(1 + Re_ε(x)) + (G(x) / (Ψ_B − Ψ_A)) · M_E^(ij)

Requires: explicit coordinates x on the parameter space; derivation of the
Re_ε and G dependence from the rheology layer; verification that the metric
is positive definite.
